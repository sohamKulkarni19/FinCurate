1. Purpose and product outcome

The product helps a user research, compare, screen, and shortlist currently investable Indian mutual funds using reproducible return and risk analytics. It is not intended to issue buy, sell, or hold recommendations.

Discover

Rank funds within a comparable universe, then narrow the result using evidence-based filters.

Understand

Inspect trailing, rolling, benchmark-relative, drawdown, and risk-adjusted performance.

Compare

Plot up to five funds, with SIP and lump-sum outcomes calculated over identical windows.

Shortlist

Save a small set of candidates and display their trade-offs without an opaque composite rating.

2. Scope

Included in version 1Excluded from version 1
Active, open-ended Direct Growth schemes; active funds, index funds, and funds of funds; NAV and normalized-growth charts; trailing returns; rolling SIP XIRR and lump-sum CAGR; fixed benchmark choices for eligible domestic-equity-oriented schemes; risk and drawdown measures; category rankings; CSV export; local shortlist.Regular plans, IDCW options, closed-ended or inactive schemes, ETFs without a Direct Growth plan, portfolio holdings analysis, fund-manager qualitative scoring, tax calculations, exit-load modelling, investment execution, personalized investment advice, cloud accounts, and public hosting.
Interpretation boundaryPast returns and statistical rankings are evidence about historical behavior, not forecasts. The interface must show this limitation near ranking and shortlist results.

3. Locked business rules

IDRuleDecision
BR-01Eligible scheme universeOnly active, open-ended schemes explicitly identified as Direct Growth. Include active, index, and FoF schemes.
BR-02Rolling return presets6 months, 1 year, 3 years, 5 years, 7 years, and 10 years. No custom rolling duration.
BR-03SIP dateUse the first available NAV on or after the first calendar day of each month.
BR-04SIP windowFor an M-month window, make M contributions at month offsets 0 through M-1 and value the accumulated units at month M.
BR-05AnnualizationSIP uses dated-cash-flow XIRR. Lump sum uses actual-day CAGR. Results below one year remain annualized and receive a warning.
BR-06Rolling frequencyStart a new observation each calendar month.
BR-07Comparable periodRankings and multi-fund statistics default to the common overlapping period. An individual full-history view is optional.
BR-08Benchmark seriesUse only Nifty 50 TRI, Nifty Next 50 TRI, Nifty 500 TRI, Nifty Midcap 150 TRI, and Nifty Smallcap 250 TRI.
BR-09Benchmark eligibilityEnable for domestic-equity-oriented funds and domestic equity FoFs. Disable for debt, hybrid, and international FoFs.
BR-10Benchmark choiceRecommend a category-appropriate benchmark, allow another eligible choice, and warn rather than block when it is unusual.
BR-11Risk-free proxyUse a validated historical Indian 91-day Treasury bill series for Sharpe and Sortino calculations. Never substitute an undisclosed constant.
BR-12Ranking philosophyRank only within a clearly identified universe. Do not create a hidden or universal composite score.

4. Primary user journeys

Choose category
Choose period
Apply evidence filters
Inspect candidates
Save shortlist

Journey A: inspect one fund

  1. Search for and select an eligible scheme.
  2. Review its normalized growth chart, NAV, trailing returns, risk metrics, and drawdowns.
  3. If eligible, choose a TRI benchmark and inspect trailing and rolling excess returns.
  4. Download the displayed data or add the fund to the local shortlist.

Journey B: compare funds

  1. Select two to five eligible funds.
  2. Choose a rolling preset and a range of commencement dates.
  3. Toggle SIP, lump sum, or both.
  4. Review common-period charts and statistics, including benchmark-relative results where applicable.

Journey C: screen a category

  1. Select a category, rolling preset, and common analysis period.
  2. Rank by median rolling SIP XIRR or another supported column.
  3. Filter by downside, consistency, risk-adjusted, and benchmark-relative measures.
  4. Compare and save the resulting candidates.

5. Functional requirements

5.1 Universe, data status, and search

FR-101

Eligible-universe enforcement

Must

The system shall expose only active, open-ended Direct Growth schemes in searches, comparisons, and rankings.

Acceptance: regular, IDCW, inactive, and closed-ended records do not appear; index funds and FoFs meeting all inclusion rules do appear.
FR-102

Scheme discovery

Must

The user shall search by scheme name or fund house and filter by current category and scheme family.

Acceptance: results display canonical name, fund house, current category, inception date, and latest NAV date.
FR-103

Data freshness and evidence

Must

Every page shall expose the latest data date. A fund with gaps, stale NAVs, unresolved category, or insufficient history shall carry a visible status and be excluded from affected rankings.

5.2 Fund explorer and trailing performance

FR-201

Fund overview

Must

The fund page shall show canonical scheme identity, fund house, category, plan and option, inception date, latest NAV, latest NAV date, available history, and benchmark eligibility.

FR-202

Growth and NAV chart

Must

The default chart shall show the growth of a nominal Rs 10,000 investment. The user may toggle to raw NAV. Eligible benchmark and comparison series shall be rebased to the same starting value.

Acceptance: hover displays date and value; date zoom is available; missing observations are not silently interpolated.
FR-203

Trailing-return table

Must

Display 1-, 3-, and 6-month absolute returns; 1-, 3-, 5-, and 10-year CAGR; and since-inception CAGR. Unsupported periods shall show "insufficient history," not zero.

FR-204

Trailing benchmark comparison

Must

For an eligible benchmark selection, display fund return, benchmark return, excess percentage points, and beat/did-not-beat status for each available trailing period.

FR-205

Category context

Should

Display the category median and the selected fund's percentile for trailing periods when a comparable category sample exists.

5.3 Rolling SIP and lump-sum analysis

FR-301

Preset selection

Must

The user shall choose exactly one preset: 6 months, 1 year, 3 years, 5 years, 7 years, or 10 years.

FR-302

Analysis modes

Must

The user shall toggle between SIP only, lump sum only, and both. SIP and lump-sum observations shall use identical start and valuation dates.

FR-303

Rolling chart

Must

Plot annualized return against investment commencement month for up to five funds. Use one color per fund, solid lines for SIP, and dashed lines for lump sum. Hover shall show fund, mode, start date, valuation date, and return.

FR-304

Date-range filtering

Must

The user shall narrow the displayed commencement-date range without changing the rolling duration.

FR-305

Rolling statistics

Must

For each fund and mode, show mean, median, standard deviation of outcomes, minimum, maximum, P10, P25, P75, P90, negative-return frequency, number of rolling observations, and number of non-overlapping windows.

FR-306

Return distribution

Must

Show the percentage of observations in clearly defined return bands. The user may inspect the underlying observations used for every count.

FR-307

Rolling benchmark comparison

Must

For eligible funds, simulate the same SIP or lump-sum cash flows against the selected TRI. Show benchmark win rate, median excess return, P10 excess return, and benchmark-relative distribution.

FR-308

Evidence warning

Must

Explain that adjacent rolling windows overlap and are not independent. Highlight limited evidence for long presets or short-lived funds.

5.4 Risk, downside, and consistency

FR-401

Risk-adjusted metrics

Must

Calculate Sharpe and Sortino ratios from monthly returns over the selected common period using the disclosed historical 91-day Treasury bill proxy.

FR-402

Benchmark-relative risk

Must

For benchmark-eligible funds, calculate upside capture, downside capture, information ratio, and tracking error from aligned monthly observations.

FR-403

Downside evidence

Must

Show maximum drawdown, worst 1-year return, worst 3-year return, drawdown start, trough, recovery date, and recovery duration where history permits.

FR-404

Underwater chart

Should

Plot percentage decline from the previous peak and allow the user to inspect historical drawdown episodes.

FR-405

Multi-period consistency

Should

Show category percentile or top-quartile status across supported rolling presets and compare recent 3- and 5-year evidence with full common history.

5.5 Screener, ranking, and shortlist

FR-501

Comparable ranking universe

Must

Rank by current category by default. An all-funds exploratory view may be provided but shall warn that asset classes are not directly comparable.

FR-502

Ranking controls

Must

The user shall choose category, rolling preset, analysis period, minimum history, and minimum observation count before results are ranked.

FR-503

Filterable evidence

Must

Filters shall include median SIP XIRR, median lump-sum CAGR, P10 return, negative-return frequency, benchmark win rate, median excess return, Sharpe, Sortino, upside capture, downside capture, information ratio, tracking error, and maximum drawdown where applicable.

FR-504

Transparent exclusions

Must

The screener shall report why a scheme was excluded: insufficient history, missing data, stale status, category mismatch, unavailable benchmark data, or failed user filter.

FR-505

Local shortlist

Must

The user shall add or remove funds from a persistent local shortlist and compare up to five shortlisted funds in a trade-off matrix.

FR-506

No hidden score

Must

The application shall not collapse all evidence into an unexplained universal score. Any category percentile or pass/fail flag shall expose its inputs and period.

5.6 Export and disclosures

FR-601

CSV export

Must

Allow export of trailing tables, rolling observations, summary statistics, and screener results currently in scope. Exports shall include calculation date, filters, benchmark, and data-through date.

FR-602

Methodology visibility

Must

Every calculated metric shall link to a plain-language definition containing formula, frequency, period, data convention, and known limitations.

FR-603

Research disclaimer

Must

Ranking and shortlist screens shall state that past performance is not indicative of future results and that the tool does not provide investment advice.

6. Functional calculation definitions

MeasureRequired behavior
SIP rolling XIRRUse equal nominal monthly outflows and one terminal inflow equal to total accumulated units multiplied by the valuation NAV. Because scaling every cash flow equally leaves XIRR unchanged, the nominal SIP amount is not a user input.
Lump-sum rolling CAGRUse actual elapsed days between selected start and valuation NAVs. Annualize every preset, including six months.
Trailing returnUse the latest valid NAV as the endpoint and the first valid NAV on or after the target historical date. Show absolute return below one year and CAGR from one year onward.
SharpeAnnualized mean monthly excess return divided by monthly excess-return standard deviation; risk-free observations must align by month.
SortinoAnnualized mean monthly excess return divided by downside deviation of negative monthly excess returns.
Capture ratiosUse fund and benchmark monthly returns on benchmark-up and benchmark-down months respectively; periods and alignment must be identical.
Information ratioAnnualized mean monthly active return divided by annualized tracking error.
Maximum drawdownLargest percentage decline from a prior peak in the normalized value series; recovery occurs when the previous peak is regained.

8. Functional quality rules

  • No missing value may silently become zero.
  • No comparison may silently use different periods.
  • No price index may be labeled or treated as a TRI benchmark.
  • No benchmark-dependent metric may appear when benchmark data is disabled or incomplete.
  • Every percentage shall identify whether it is absolute, annualized, excess percentage points, or a frequency.
  • Every chart shall expose the calculation window through labels or hover content.
  • All-fund rankings shall remain explicitly exploratory; category ranking is the decision-oriented default.
  • Filters shall use inclusive boundary behavior and show the active values above the results.

9. Version 1 release acceptance

Release gateVersion 1 is functionally acceptable only when every Must requirement has a passing test, the prerequisite checklist has no unresolved blocker, and independently calculated fixtures agree within the documented tolerance.
  • A user can find every eligible test scheme and none of the deliberately ineligible fixtures.
  • All six rolling presets produce reproducible SIP and lump-sum observations.
  • Five-fund comparison uses a provable common period and remains legible at laptop width.
  • Eligible equity funds can be compared against all five validated TRI datasets.
  • Debt, hybrid, and international FoF pages hide benchmark-dependent metrics without hiding independent risk metrics.
  • The screener can reduce a large category result through multiple filters and explain every exclusion.
  • Exports reproduce the visible filters, dates, source versions, and summary values.
  • The application remains usable without internet access after datasets have been refreshed and cached.

Requirements marked Should may move to a later iteration if they do not compromise calculation integrity or the primary shortlisting journey.
